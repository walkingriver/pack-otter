Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Summary

PackOtter stores your trips and packing lists on your device only. We do not require an account. We do not sell data. We do not use analytics SDKs in v1.

Data stored on your device

  • Trip names, dates, and packing preferences
  • Checklist items (labels, quantities, checked state)
  • App preferences (onboarding complete, Pro status)

Data sent over the network

Play Billing: Google processes purchases; we receive purchase state only.

PackOtter does not upload your trips or packing lists to our servers.

Data we do not collect

  • Location / GPS (v1)
  • Contacts, photos, or notification content
  • Advertising identifiers

Export and deletion

  • Export any trip as JSON from the trip menu
  • Export all data from Settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data

Contact

Walking River Software — privacy inquiries via GitHub issues on this repository.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in this document and the in-app privacy link.
