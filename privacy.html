Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary

PackOtter stores your trips and packing lists on your device only. We do not require an account. We do not sell data. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data stored on your device

  • Trip names, dates, and packing preferences
  • Traveler names you enter (for per-person lists)
  • "Before you leave" choices (for example: flying, pets, plants)
  • Checklist items (labels, quantities, checked state)
  • App preferences (onboarding complete, Pro status)

Data sent over the network

Play Billing: Google processes purchases; we receive purchase state only.

RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to check whether you bought Pro. When the app starts or you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your Google Play purchase token and receipt details, device type, OS version, app version, and country/currency. It does not receive your trips or packing lists. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

PackOtter does not upload your trips or packing lists to our servers.

Sharing a list

When you share a traveler's list, PackOtter puts that list inside the link itself (in the ?p= part). We do not store it. Anyone who has the link can read the list, so share it only with people you trust.

If the person you send it to opens the link in a web browser, their browser loads a small static page from this website so it can hand the list to PackOtter. The website is hosted by Cloudflare and GitHub, which may keep standard web server logs (such as IP address and the requested URL) under their own privacy policies. The page itself does not save or send the list anywhere.

Data we do not collect

  • Location / GPS
  • Contacts, photos, or notification content
  • Advertising identifiers

Deletion

  • Delete a trip in the app to remove it from your device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • To ask RevenueCat to delete purchase records tied to your random app user ID, contact us (below).

Contact

Walking River Software — privacy questions: open an issue on GitHub.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in this document and the in-app privacy link.
